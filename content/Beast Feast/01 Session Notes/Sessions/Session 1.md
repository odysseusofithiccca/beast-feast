## Plot Hook
It is the annual [[Stewpot Festival]], celebrating the day [[Hylaeus the Forest Mage]] protected the town of Bellmouth by driving the beasts of the land into [[Maja-Suma (The Great Pit)|The Great Pit]], allowing the village to prosper and grow into the food hub it is today. Everyone is having a good time and celebrating, until Erika Champagne starts a war... of sorts.
## Key Scenes

> Long ago, within the heart of the Great Pit, Hylaeus the Forest Mage (she/her) cast a powerful spell that draws dangerous creatures from the surrounding land into the caverns. For centuries, this kept any harm from coming to the village of Bellmouth. 
> 
> However, the caves are now filled to the brim with strange, unexpected creatures from across the region—and the remnants of outsiders brave or foolhardy enough to venture into them. But nobody from Bellmouth is foolish enough to be caught dead in the caves. 
> 
> The quiet, rustic village is full of bakers and weavers, farmers and cobblers, with ne’er a sword in sight. The villagers are more concerned about not mucking up their contribution to the yearly[[ Stewpot Festival]] than the dangers outside their walls.
> 
> As springtime comes into full effect, the villagers are doing their best to make sure this year's festivities are more loud, more grand, and more delicious than last year's. Large colourful balloons in the shapes of various flying beasts paint the sky, while pink, yellow and blue wildflowers decorate the verdant valley below. The air is filled with happy songs and the sweet smell of cinnamon, butter and sugar being made into all sorts of treats. Mind you, the actual festival is in 3 moons. Everyone in Bellmouth is getting ready to party... well, almost everyone.
