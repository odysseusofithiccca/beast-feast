Bellmouth is a town in the nation of [[Iadifan]] on [[The Ageless Earth]]. 

It is the closest town to the [[Maja-Suma (The Great Pit)|Maja-Suma]].

The people of Bellmouth have lived peacefully for generations. Thanks to [[Hylaeus the Forest Mage|Hylaeus]], the region’s dangerous creatures are enticed to the caves; meanwhile, other towns or armies have largely left the unassuming village alone except as a pit stop for adventurers exploring the caves.
